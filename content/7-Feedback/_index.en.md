### General Evaluation

**1. Work Environment**  
The work environment is quite friendly and open. The members of FCAJ are always ready to support when I face difficulties, even outside of working hours.

**2. Support from mentors / team admin**  
The mentors provided very thorough guidance, explained clearly when I didn't understand, and always encouraged me to ask questions. The team admin supported with procedures, documents, and created favorable conditions for me to work smoothly. They were willing to provide feedback on my project even after 10:00 PM.

**3. Alignment between the work and my major**  
The explosion of AI has caused significant shifts in the IT industry. Among these, Cloud Service Providers (CSPs) like AWS, Azure, and GCP are becoming the trend. Therefore, interning at FCAJ gave me the opportunity to get hands-on experience with Cloud services, thereby enhancing my specialized knowledge and skills in line with current trends.

**4. Opportunities for learning & skill development**  
During the internship, I learned many new skills such as using AWS tools, teamwork skills, and professional communication in a corporate environment. The mentors also shared a lot of practical experience, helping me better orient my future studies and career development.

**5. Culture & team spirit**  
The company culture is very positive: everyone respects each other, working seriously but still having fun. When there is an urgent project, everyone strives together and supports each other regardless of position. This makes me feel like a part of the team, even though I am just an intern.

**6. Policies / benefits for interns**  
The job referral benefit for interns sounds very attractive, although I haven't been able to use it yet. In addition, the opportunity to interact with senior DevOps engineers is a huge plus.
