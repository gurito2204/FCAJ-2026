During my internship at **Amazon Web Services Viet Nam Company Limited (Bootcamp First Cloud AI Journey)** from **June 16, 2026**, to **July 31, 2026**, I had the opportunity to learn, practice, and apply the knowledge acquired at school in a real-world working environment.  
I participated in an **intensive cloud computing training program and directly collaborated on deploying a practical project (a B2C E-commerce Website) onto AWS infrastructure**, thereby improving my skills in **Cloud infrastructure deployment and management, teamwork, system testing, and analytical and problem-solving skills**.

Regarding professional conduct, I always strived to complete my tasks well, strictly adhered to the rules, and actively communicated with colleagues to enhance work efficiency.

### Knowledge and Skills Acquired

Following the internship and participating in the Bootcamp over the past 7 weeks, I have learned, gained practical experience, and acquired a wealth of valuable knowledge and skills, specifically:

- **Fundamental and Advanced Knowledge of Cloud Computing (AWS):** Gained a clear understanding of cloud computing principles compared to traditional (on-premise) servers. Proficient in configuring and utilizing core AWS services (EC2, S3, RDS, VPC) as well as advanced services for system automation and monitoring (EC2 Auto Scaling, Amazon CloudWatch, Route 53, AWS CLI).
- **Hands-on Experience in Practical Project Deployment:** Directly participated in the deployment process of a complete B2C E-commerce Website. Learned how to deploy the Frontend onto AWS infrastructure, configure routing, synchronize connections with Backend APIs, and conduct testing on user access flows to ensure smooth system operation.
- **Security Management and Cost Optimization Skills:** Mastered security and system authorization principles using AWS IAM (creating users, groups, and switching roles). Concurrently, learned how to set up AWS Budgets to control and optimize costs when running projects on the Cloud.
- **Teamwork and Research Skills:** Enhanced brainstorming capabilities for project ideas, coordinating seamlessly with the Backend team to resolve integration issues. Honed independent research skills by referencing open-source projects on GitHub and AWS documentation to discover the most optimal deployment solutions.
- **Applying Theory to Practice:** Had the opportunity to bridge the gap between academic knowledge (computer networks, databases) and real-world application by deploying VPCs, RDS, etc. This helped identify my shortcomings in system architecture mindset, allowing me to plan for further improvement.
